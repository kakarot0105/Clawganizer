# Clawganizer - Marketing & Messaging

## 30-Second Pitch

**Clawganizer** is a free OpenClaw skill that organizes your development workspace automatically. Create projects, structure folders, and manage everything from Telegram or command line. Perfect for developers juggling multiple projects.

## Elevator Pitch (2 Minutes)

Are you managing multiple development projects and getting lost in folder structures? Clawganizer solves that.

It's a free, open-source OpenClaw skill that:
- **Creates** projects with one command
- **Organizes** your workspace automatically
- **Archives** old projects to keep things clean
- **Tracks** your workspace status

Just type `/clawganizer "create project MyApp"` and you're done. That's it.

Perfect for solo developers, teams, and anyone using OpenClaw.

## Why Clawganizer Matters

### The Problem
- Developers waste time managing folder structures
- Projects get buried or forgotten
- No standardized workspace organization
- Hard to onboard new team members

### The Solution
- **One command** to create organized projects
- **Automatic structure** - no manual setup
- **Simple commands** - easy to learn and teach
- **Open source** - free forever
